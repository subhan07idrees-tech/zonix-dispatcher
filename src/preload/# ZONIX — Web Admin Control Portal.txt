### Install on Smartphone (PWA)
Add ZONIX Web Admin to your phone's home screen as a standalone mobile app.
1. Open https://thezonix.com/admin in Safari.
2. Tap the Share icon (bottom toolbar).
3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
1. Open https://thezonix.com/admin in Chrome.
2. Tap the 3 dots menu (top right).
3. Tap Install app or Add to Home Screen.
